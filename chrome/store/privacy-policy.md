# View for Chrome: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-19_

View is a document viewer that runs entirely on your computer. It has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What View accesses, and why

- **Documents you open.** When you open a PDF or another file kind you have chosen in View's Preferences, View downloads it again from the same address, the way Chrome would, using your browser's cookies and the original page as referrer, so that it can show it to you. Local files you open, drop or pick are read on your computer. Documents are processed only in your browser.
- **Pages with embedded PDFs.** View's content script looks for PDFs embedded in web pages so it can show them in View. It reads nothing else on the page.
- **Settings.** Your preferences (which file kinds open in View, and the theme) are saved with Chrome's extension storage. If Chrome sync is on, Chrome syncs them across your devices.
- **Signatures and recent files.** Signatures you create, and the list of recently opened files, are stored locally in the extension's storage on your device. The recent list holds file handles that Chrome manages, not copies of your files.
- **Text recognition (Live Text).** It runs locally with models included in the extension. No image or text is sent anywhere.

## What View does not do

View does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, browsing history or document content. It makes no network requests except to download the documents you open, from the addresses they came from.

## Removing data

Uninstalling View removes its settings, signatures and recent-files list from your browser. You can also clear the recent-files list from View's start screen at any time.
