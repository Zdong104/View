# Chrome Web Store listing (draft)

**Name:** View

**Summary** (132 characters max):
A Preview-style viewer for PDFs, images, 3D models, tables and text: view, annotate and sign right in Chrome.

**Category:** Productivity (Tools as an alternative)

**Language:** English

## Description

View opens your documents in a clean, fast viewer modelled on macOS Preview, right inside Chrome. Use it for PDFs and, if you like, for images, 3D models, spreadsheets, text files and video.

**PDF**
- Fast on huge files: pages stream in as you scroll, so a 1,000-page document opens instantly.
- Thumbnails, search, text selection, form filling and printing.
- Delete, reorder, rotate and insert pages, or merge several PDFs into one.
- Save only the pages you selected, export pages as images, protect a PDF with a password, or reduce its file size.

**Markup and signatures**
- Sketch, draw shapes and arrows, add text boxes and notes, and highlight, underline or strike through text, on PDFs and images.
- Sign with your mouse, trackpad, pen or finger. Signatures are stored on your device as vectors, so they stay sharp.

**Images**
- Zoom, rotate, flip, crop, adjust colour, resize, and remove backgrounds with Instant Alpha.
- See EXIF and GPS details.
- Animated GIF, WebP and APNG files keep their animation when you edit them.
- Combine images into a PDF, or convert many images at once.

**Live Text**
- Select and copy text in images and scanned PDFs, in English and Chinese.
- Text recognition runs entirely on your computer.

**More formats**
- 3D models (STL, OBJ, PLY) with orbit controls, dimensions, volume and surface area.
- CSV and TSV tables with millions of rows, which you can also edit.
- Text and source code with syntax highlighting.
- Video and audio.

**Make View your default**
In Preferences, choose which kinds of files open in View: PDFs (on by default), images, 3D models, tables, text and video. You can always open a file in Chrome's own viewer instead from the menu.

**Private by design**
View has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your documents never leave your computer.

**Local files**
To open PDFs from your computer by clicking them or dragging them into Chrome, turn on "Allow access to file URLs" for View on the extensions page. You can also use **Open…** or drag files onto a View tab without that setting.
